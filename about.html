About

A jacket-first fashion studio focused on utility, proportion and a more thoughtful digital apparel experience.

Studio

Function,
edited beautifully.

AEROWARD brings fashion direction and product thinking together. We care about the small decisions: a shoulder line, a cuff, a zipper pull, a useful pocket, a fabric that changes character under light.

Principles

Less noise.
More intent.

We use a small set of principles to keep apparel work clear, useful and visually strong.

FORMUseful formEvery panel, pocket and closure earns its place through function.
MATERIALMaterial honestyTextures, weight and finish are presented clearly so the garment feels tangible.
FITHuman fitProportion follows real movement, layering and the person wearing the jacket.
